Safety Standards & Reporting

Last updated: 27 October 2025

All apps within social or dating categories must provide published safety standards and contact information to comply with our child safety standards policy.

Published Safety Standards

DoorLah is committed to providing a safe experience for all users. Our published safety standards include:

  • Clear rules and community guidelines for acceptable behaviour.
  • Reporting mechanisms for abuse, harassment, or exploitation.
  • Age restrictions and measures to prevent underage use where applicable.
  • Transparent moderation and escalation procedures.
  • Privacy protections and secure handling of user data.

Report Issues, Abuse, or Concerns

If you encounter behaviour or content that violates our safety standards, or if you have any other concerns, please contact us immediately so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Email: doorlah.singapore@gmail.com

What to include when you report

  • A short description of the issue.
  • Any relevant usernames, profile links, or community names.
  • Time, date, and screenshots or copies of messages if available.
  • Your preferred contact email so we can follow up (optional).

We take all reports seriously and will respond as soon as possible. For urgent safety threats, please contact your local emergency services first.
