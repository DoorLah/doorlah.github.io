# DoorLah Website Generation Brief 🏘️

This document serves as a comprehensive instruction set for a coding agent to generate a premium, modern landing page for the **DoorLah** app.

## 🌟 Project Vision

DoorLah is a community-focused mobile application designed for Singapore housing communities (HDB, condominiums, and private estates). It connects neighbors, facilitates local commerce, and enhances community engagement.

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Design System

The website should reflect the app's modern, premium, and trustworthy aesthetic.

### Typography

- **Primary Font**: `Poppins` (Google Fonts). Use Bold for headers and Medium/Regular for body.

### Color Palette (Navy Blue Theme)

- **Primary**: `#1A237E` (Dark Navy Blue)
- **Primary Gradient**: From `#283593` to `#1A237E`
- **Background**: `#F8F9FA` (Light mode) / `#0D1117` (Dark mode)
- **Surface/Cards**: `#FFFFFF` (Light) / `#161B22` (Dark)
- **Feature Colors**:
  - **Chat**: `#64B5F6` (Metallic Blue)
  - **Services**: `#81C784` (Metallic Green)
  - **Marketplace**: `#FFB74D` (Metallic Orange)
  - **Shops**: `#BA68C8` (Metallic Purple)

### Design Requirements

- **Premium Aesthetics**: Use glassmorphism, subtle micro-animations, and smooth transitions.
- **Responsive**: Mobile-first design that looks stunning on tablets and desktops.
- **Custom Elements**: The signature "Circular Orange Home Button" should be represented as a key call-to-action (CTA).
- **Icons**: Use modern, thin-stroke icons (HeroIcons or Lucide).

## 🧩 Website Structure

### 1. Hero Section

- **Headline**: "Your Neighborhood, Connected."
- **Sub-headline**: "The all-in-one community hub for Singapore's HDBs, Condominiums, and Private Estates."
- **CTA**: "Download DoorLah" (Primary Button) and "Explore Features" (Secondary).
- **Visual**: A high-fidelity mockup of the app’s Home Tab showing the "Explore" grid.

### 2. Feature Grid (The "Explore" Section)

Highlight the four main pillars using gradients and icons matching the app:

- **Community Chat**: (Metallic Blue) Real-time messaging with your neighbors. Share updates, announcements, and stay informed. "Connect and communicate in real-time."
- **Notice Board**: (Navy Blue) "Stay updated with the latest community announcements, events, and important notices."
- **Marketplace**: (Metallic Orange) "Buy, sell, or rent items, rooms, and homes within your community." Browse categories like grocery, electronics, and furniture.
- **Local Services & Shops**: (Metallic Green/Purple) "Discover trusted service providers and neighborhood shops at your doorstep." Book plumbers, cleaners, or browse local F&B.

### 3. Business Mode Spotlight

- **Headline**: "Your Passion, Your Business, Your Neighborhood."
- **Content**: Showcase how residents can toggle to "Business Mode" to manage their own shops and services.
- **Key Point**: "Instant digital storefronts for community-based commerce."

### 4. Fast & Reliable (The Tech Edge)

- **Slogan**: "Instant Load. Offline Ready."
- **Content**: Highlight the smart caching system that ensures 90-95% faster performance and works even without internet.

### 5. Singapore Community Focus

- Mention specific estate types: HDB, Condos, and Private Estates.
- Tagline: "Made with ❤️ for Singapore Communities."

### 6. Footer

- Links to Socials, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service.
- Brief About section.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack Recommendations

- **Frontend**: Vite + React or Next.js (for performance and SEO).
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS or TailwindCSS with a custom theme configuration.
- **Animations**: Framer Motion or GSAP for that "premium" feel.

## 🤖 Instructions for the Coding Agent

> "Using the details above, create a high-fidelity landing page for DoorLah. Prioritize visual excellence. Use rich gradients, glassmorphism for cards, and hover effects that feel responsive and alive. Ensure the primary navy blue theme is dominant but accented by the feature-specific metallic colors. The final result should look like a world-class SaaS landing page."
