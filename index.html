verified_userTrust and safety

Built for the people next door.

DoorLah only works if neighbours feel safe. These are the rules and tools that keep it that way.

gavel

Standards come first

Everyone agrees to the Terms of Use and Community Standards before they see their neighbours' posts.

flag

Report or block, anywhere

On messages, notices, listings, services and shops. We review every report within 24 hours.

location_on

Tied to your block

Your postal code places you in your block's community. Owners can add their tenants.

mark_email_read

Verified sign-up

New accounts confirm their email before they can sign in.

cloud_off

Works on patchy signal

Saved chats, notices and listings stay viewable when you're offline.

delete

Leave whenever you like

Delete your account from your profile at any time. Your listings and shops are taken down with it.
