Request Account Deletion

Follow these steps to request deletion of your DoorLah account and associated personal data.

We're sad to see you go

If you still want to proceed, we'll help you remove your account and personal data. Please send us a request and we'll verify your identity before processing.

You can email us directly at doorlah.singapore@gmail.com

Suggested email subject: Account Deletion Request - [Your Full Name]

Account Deletion Request - [Your Full Name]

What to include in your request

To help us verify and process your request quickly, include the following information in your email:

  • Your full name
  • The email address associated with your DoorLah account
  • Your DoorLah username (if applicable)
  • The community/estate you belong to (e.g., HDB block / condo name)
  • A short reason for the deletion request (optional)

Identity verification

For security and to prevent unauthorized deletion, we may ask you to verify your identity. Typical verification steps may include:

  • Confirming a verification code sent to your registered email or phone number
  • Responding from the email address associated with your account

What will be deleted

Once we verify your request, we will remove or anonymize personal data tied to your account where possible. This may include:

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone, profile photo)
  • Your listings (marketplace/shop) — these may be removed or marked as from a deleted user
  • Your personal messages and direct content — retention may vary where required by law or for safety reasons

Retention exceptions

Some information may be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons (for example, to comply with law enforcement requests, prevent fraud, or preserve transaction records). We will only retain the minimum data necessary for those purposes.

Processing time

We aim to respond to deletion requests within 30 days from verification. Complex requests may take longer; we will keep you informed by email.

Need more help?

If you have questions about the deletion process or need assistance, contact us at doorlah.singapore@gmail.com or return to our Privacy Policy for more details.
